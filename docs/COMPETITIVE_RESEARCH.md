## Feature gaps closed in this rebuild

- Live docket of **pending** WIPO filings with estimated response deadlines (none of the free tools surface this with deadlines).
- Fully automated daily refresh of decisions, docket, statistics, daily brief and sitemap.
- Per-case pages enriched with domain anatomy, complainant history and plain-language outcome explanations.
- Interactive tools: deadline calculator, fee estimator (WIPO / Forum / CAC 2026 schedules), RDAP domain lookup, typo-variant scanner, pre-check.
- Dedicated lead generation (/get-help), sponsorship / advertising / partnership intake, donations, contests with official rules, careers talent pool.
- AdSense-ready structure: About, Contact, Privacy (ads + cookies), Terms, Disclaimer, Cookie policy, Trademark & copyright disclosure, ads.txt, sitemap, robots.
